❓ What It Does

What Does This Project Actually Do?

Turning a confusing solar decision into a simple, data-backed one — using algebra.

🎯 The Short Answer

You enter your electricity usage → the system calculates the exact number of solar panels you need, the cost savings, and the environmental benefit — instantly.

The Problem It Solves

📈

Rising Bills

Electricity costs keep climbing, but most households guess at solar sizing instead of calculating it.

🤷

No Simple Tool

Families rarely have an easy way to match their real energy use to the right panel count.

💸

Missed Savings

Wrong-sized systems waste money — either overspending on panels or under-covering usage.

What The System Outputs

🔢 Panels Required

The exact number of solar panels needed to meet the household's daily energy demand.

💰 Estimated Electricity Cost

Projects current electricity spend based on entered consumption and local tariff rates (₹8/kWh).

✅ Annual Savings

Calculates how much the household saves each year after switching to solar power.

🌍 Environmental Benefit

Estimates the reduction in CO₂ emissions from replacing grid power with solar energy.

Solar Smart Home proves that algebra isn't just a subject in a textbook — it's a real tool that helps families cut costs, save energy, and protect the environment.
